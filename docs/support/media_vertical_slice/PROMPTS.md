# MV02 image-generation prompts

Mode for every direction: built-in image generation, new image,
`stylized-concept`. No input image or third-party source was used.

## Direction A - threshold

```text
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: Gallicus 2.0 game environment keyframe, MV02 Direction A
Primary request: A first-person view standing at the administrative arena threshold at dusk, looking through a severe monumental entrance toward a sealed Registry table beyond the threshold. The Registry table is the central ritual-administrative object and the only clear focal point.
Scene/backdrop: A restrained Roman civic arena interior designed as an austere administrative tribunal, built from dark basalt blocks and oxidized bronze architectural fittings. The central floor is empty, functional, geometrically ordered, and usable for gameplay. Sparse airborne dust and narrow light shafts reveal scale without filling the space.
Subject: The sealed Registry table sits centered on the far axis beyond the entrance, unmistakable as a heavy official object but carrying no readable writing, symbols, seals, logos, or ornament that could be interpreted as text.
Style/medium: cinematic 2D/2.5D painterly game environment concept art, production keyframe, grounded material realism, broad shapes with controlled detail, suitable for later separation into depth layers.
Composition/framing: exact 16:9 landscape composition, first-person human eye height, threshold architecture framing the foreground, strong symmetrical administrative axis, clear depth planes, empty functional center, Registry table visible and readable at medium distance. No visible player body or hands.
Lighting/mood: late dusk, cool charcoal ambient light with restrained warm oxidized-bronze reflections, sparse volumetric shafts, solemn procedural ritual, quiet institutional tension rather than horror or adventure.
Color palette: basalt charcoal, ash gray, muted umber, verdigris and oxidized bronze, very restrained dusty amber highlights.
Materials/textures: weighty Roman basalt, aged hammered bronze, worn stone floor, controlled dry dust; no parchment surfaces.
Constraints: no people, no characters, no statues presented as characters, no gore, no blood, no weapons, no fantasy magic, no supernatural glow, no parchment, no scrolls, no readable text, no letters, no numerals, no logos, no watermark. No action pose or action-film energy. Preserve a sober Roman administrative ritual identity and an object-first visual hierarchy.
```
