## Purpose

Entries maintain stable nomenclature, cross-reference continuity, and unambiguous administrative framing. Arena protocol appears as ordinary institutional condition rather than declared exception. Owl administration is recorded as non-reactive custodianship, with no supplementary commentary beyond ordinary registral maintenance.

## II. Era of the Rigid Register
Subsequent strata indicate a Register with reduced tolerance to deviation in form and categorization. Structural convergence appears earlier in record formation, and semantic ambiguity is narrowed through constrained lexical recurrence. Archival continuity remains present but less permissive in interpretive variance.

## Second Silence
The second Silence is indexed as premature saturation of registral process. Internal evidence suggests a system response to accumulated precedent density. The event remains documented in impersonal terms, without attribution to agentic intent.

## III. Era of the Unstable Register
Later strata display lexical fractures across homologous entry classes. Protocol behavior appears asymmetrical between comparable records, and administrative rhythm is inconsistently distributed across adjacent sequences. Owl administration is documented as managing ongoing instability conditions rather than exerting comprehensive control. No adversarial personalization is present in preserved notation.

## Third Silence
The third Silence is recorded as a structural event within the stratified sequence rather than as an anomaly external to it. ## IV. Terminal Register
The terminal stratum is marked by rarefied language and minimal commentary. Administrative presence persists in formulaic notation, but interpretive framing is withdrawn. Entries remain registral in form while function approaches a null state of classificatory expression.
