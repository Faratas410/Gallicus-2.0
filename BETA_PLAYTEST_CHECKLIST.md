# Gallicus v0.5 Internal Beta Playtest Checklist

## Setup
- Use the latest internal beta build.
- Start from a clean launch.
- Record platform, resolution, input method, and whether audio is enabled.

## Core Loop
- Launch reaches main menu without fatal error.
- Objective is understandable without external explanation.
- New run starts from menu.
- Betting book opens and presents at least two readable choices.
- Signing a bet produces visual, audio, and UI feedback.
- Pact ritual opens, can be advanced, and closes.
- Intermediate choice opens and both choices are understandable.
- Resolve ritual opens, accepts ritual strikes/advance, and closes.
- Push-your-luck opens with clear cashout/double/condanna consequences.
- END_RUN finale is clear and includes restart/next/menu route.

## Session Test
- Complete 3 runs in one executable session.
- Use at least one cashout path.
- Use at least one double path.
- Use at least one condanna path.
- Confirm settings can open/close from menu.
- Confirm audio feedback is present for menu, bet, ritual, choice, push-your-luck, and finale actions.

## Failure Watch
- No fatal errors.
- No missing texture or missing audio warnings visible to tester.
- No stuck modal.
- No unreadable or corrupted text.
- No button route that leaves the tester unable to continue or exit.
